04Where I was wrong

Four that didn't work

01
Shipped three Claude Code hooks that never ran, for two releases. A silent no-op is indistinguishable from success at a glance.
02
My benchmark lied to me — headless Chrome's 500px floor faked the mobile clipping. I published the correction on top of my own results.
03
My liveness probe kill-looped nine healthy agents. The monitoring was the incident.
04
"Calibration on N=2 isn't calibration" — so I wrote my own coverage gaps into the README before anyone else could.
