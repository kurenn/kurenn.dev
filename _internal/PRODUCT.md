# Kuri — kurenn.dev

register: brand

## Product purpose
Abraham Kuri's personal site. A builder's worldview hub: it states a belief ("AI will make the world better"), shows the four systems he is building toward that future (DevLoop, Olympus, Coba, Claude Prototype), points to the open-source work, and invites people to talk about AI and software. Funnel: Home → Manifesto → Projects → Open Source → Contact.

## Users
Developers, operators and founders who arrive from X, GitHub or a shared link, usually on a laptop after hours or on a phone. They are technical, skeptical of AI hype, and decide in seconds whether this person is building real things or just commenting.

## Brand & tone
Technical, optimistic, first-person, precise, unhurried. The voice says "I believe…" and backs it with shipped work. Dense information is fine; decoration is not. Mono labels carry every count, status, path and date.

## Anti-references
- Not a corporate portfolio or agency site (no client-logo wall, no "services").
- Not a product sales page; each project gets a promise and a link, the product sites do the converting.
- No dark-SaaS purple/blue glow, no gradient text, no neon on pure black, no AI-sparkle iconography.
- No accent color: ink on bone, nothing else. Status, links and buttons are all ink.
- Not a résumé timeline.

## Strategic principles
- The belief comes first and every project reads as a way of acting on it, never as four unrelated things.
- Proof over adjectives: real repos, real versions, real commit dates.
- The homepage is one screen on desktop; the manifesto opens over it rather than pushing it down.
